# Firewatch Terms

Firewatch is provided as a moderation workflow tool for Reddit communities.

## Moderator Control

Firewatch provides deterministic risk signals and moderator workflow actions.
Moderators remain responsible for reviewing context and deciding whether to
remove comments, lock posts, post reminders, or take other moderation action.

## No Automated Enforcement Guarantee

Firewatch is not a replacement for moderator judgment, AutoModerator, Reddit
sitewide enforcement, or community rules. Scores are advisory and may be
incorrect or incomplete.

## Acceptable Use

Use Firewatch only for legitimate subreddit moderation and community safety
workflows. Do not use it to harass users, expose private information, or make
moderation decisions that violate Reddit policies.

## Availability

Firewatch is provided as-is. The app may change, experience downtime, or be
removed from a community at any time.

## Contact

For support, contact the app maintainer through the Reddit account listed on
the app's developer page.
