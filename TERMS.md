## What Firewatch Is

Firewatch is a moderation workflow tool. It helps moderators detect escalating
posts, review public Reddit activity, coordinate ownership, run configurable
response rules, take Reddit-native mod actions, and save handoff notes.

Firewatch is not a Reddit safety enforcement system, legal advisor, or
replacement for moderator judgment. Scores, labels, suggestions, rule matches,
and summaries are workflow aids for moderators.

Firewatch provides deterministic signals and action tools, but moderators remain

You are responsible for any moderation action you choose to run through

## Automation And Response Rules

Firewatch includes configurable Response Rules. Depending on how a moderator
configures a rule, Firewatch may suggest actions, prepare actions for approval,
auto-run safe Firewatch-only actions, or auto-run all selected actions.

Use automatic actions carefully. Test rules in a sandbox subreddit before
enabling them in an active community. Do not configure rules to make moderation
decisions that are discriminatory, retaliatory, deceptive, abusive, or
inconsistent with Reddit policies or your community rules.

Firewatch cannot guarantee that any Reddit action will succeed. Actions may
fail because of permissions, deleted content, API limits, platform changes, or
other conditions outside the app's control.
