# Firewatch Privacy Policy

Firewatch is a Reddit Devvit moderation app for installed communities.

## Data Collected

Firewatch may store:

- subreddit names and IDs for installed communities
- post IDs and comment IDs related to incidents
- public Reddit usernames involved in incident signals
- public comment excerpts used for moderator review
- moderator configuration, including keywords, suspicious domains, and score
  thresholds
- moderator actions taken through Firewatch
- handoff and final notes generated by the app

Firewatch does not collect passwords, private messages, payment data, or data
from communities where it is not installed.

## How Data Is Used

Stored data is used to display queue state, explain review scores, coordinate
moderator response, and generate handoff/final notes for moderators in the
installed community.

## Data Sharing

Firewatch does not sell data or share stored incident data with third parties.
Data is processed within Reddit's Developer Platform services and shown to
authorized moderators through the app.

## Data Retention

Queue records are retained to support moderator review and handoff. When Reddit
post or comment delete triggers are received, Firewatch deletes stored content
for that post or comment from Redis. Community moderators can remove the app
from their subreddit to stop new collection.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app maintainer through the Reddit account
listed on the app's developer page.
